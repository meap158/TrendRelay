# Multi-account management policy for TikTok and other platforms (PM-210, DRAFT)

The post behind this card ("unlimited TikTok accounts from one email") could not be read. A method for creating many accounts from
one email, or for avoiding platform detection, is mass or automated account creation and evasion; platforms prohibit it, and
TrendRelay will not build it. This note records what TrendRelay does support.

## Supported
- Several real accounts that belong to the owner or to clients who authorised them, each created by a person with its own
  identity details (including its own email) and its own login.
- Publishing through each platform's official API or official scheduling tools, with that account's own authorisation (OAuth).
- Per-account schedules, captions and content variety; no identical content blasted across all accounts.
- Per-account rate limits and pauses, and a switch to stop everything.

## Not supported (not built)
- Creating accounts in bulk or by script; email aliasing to dodge uniqueness checks.
- Rotating proxies, device fingerprint spoofing, or other tricks to avoid detection or bans.
- Buying, renting or selling accounts, or fake engagement.

## Why
Accounts created or run this way risk bans that lose the whole audience, and breach the platforms' terms. Content variety across
real accounts is the durable approach.

## Needs the owner
If a specific legitimate multi-account setup is intended (for example one brand with several regional accounts), say which
platforms and how many, and I will check the official publishing options for each.
